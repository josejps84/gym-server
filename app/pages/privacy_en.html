Privacy policy for Giimme

Last updated: $updated

Summary

Giimme works without an account, and then everything stays on your phone. If you create an account, we keep a copy of your data to sync it and for the social features. We don't sell your data or show ads. Unless you turn it off, the app sends usage data to help us improve it.

What data we process

  • Account: your email and your password (stored hashed by our sign-in provider). If you sign in with Google, we receive the name and photo of that account.
  • Profile: username, name, bio and profile photo.
  • Training: your routines, your custom exercises and your finished workouts. The workout in progress and your body weight never leave the phone.
  • Social: who you follow, who follows you, who you block and the reports you send.
  • Technical data: when you use the API, our server logs the IP address and the request. Those logs are deleted after 30 days.
  • Usage data: unless you turn it off, which screens you open and what you do in the app (for example, starting or finishing a workout), along with the app version, the phone's system, your language, your level and where you train. It comes with a random identifier of the phone and, if you have an account, its internal identifier; never your email, your name, your weights or what you type. You can turn it off when you start using the app or at any time under Settings → Data → Share usage data; whatever hasn't been sent is deleted.

What other people see

Profiles are public to the app's users: username, name, bio, photo and follower count. They also see what you choose to share: the routines you mark "Show on my profile" and the workouts you share in the feed (name, date, duration and number of sets, never the weights). When you start following someone, your followers see it in their feed. Only you see your followers and following lists.

Why we use it

  • To give you access to your account and keep your data in sync across devices.
  • For the social features: profiles, following, the feed and shared routines.
  • To send you the emails you need (confirming the account and resetting the password).
  • To review reports and protect users from abuse.
  • To understand how the app is used and decide what to improve (unless you turned it off).

The legal basis is providing the service you ask for when you create the account; for safety and moderation, our legitimate interest in keeping the app safe; and, for usage data, our legitimate interest in improving the app, which you can object to at any time by turning it off.

Providers

We use these providers, which process the data on our behalf. Their servers are in the United States:

  • Supabase: sign-in, database and profile photos.
  • Google Cloud: the API server and its logs.
  • Resend: sending emails.
  • PostHog: usage data, unless you turn it off. It is deleted after 12 months.
  • Google: only if you choose to sign in with Google.

How long we keep it

As long as you have the account. You can delete it in the app under Settings → Account → Delete account. That immediately removes your profile, your synced data, your photos and your user. The data on your phone stays there until you uninstall the app. Reports other people made about you are deleted with your account.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export, object to the use of, or delete your data. You can change your profile in the app and export your workouts from Settings. For anything else, write to the contact above. You can also complain to the data protection authority of your country.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 16. If you think a child has given us their data, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, if the change is important, tell you in the app.
